What kinds of equipment can we discuss?

The project scope includes laptops, desktops, servers, networking equipment, storage media, and other enterprise IT assets. Share your inventory so the team can confirm eligibility, handling requirements, and the right disposition path.

Can you help us recover value from retired hardware?

Remarketing and buyback are core parts of the planned offering. Eligibility and value depend on ownership, model, age, condition, specifications, market demand, and agreed costs. Any estimate must be confirmed through inspection and written terms.

What happens to the data on our equipment?

Before an engagement, define the data-bearing media, your required methods, verification, exceptions, and evidence. The approved scope determines the sanitization or destruction pathway. No certification or regulatory outcome is assumed from a website description.

What do you need to scope a project?

A useful starting point is the equipment type and approximate quantity, locations, desired timing, data-handling requirements, and your priorities for recovery and reporting. You do not need a perfect inventory to start a conversation.

Is Phoenix Tech Refresh part of iT1?

Yes. Phoenix Tech Refresh is an iT1 company. The brand combines a focused technology-lifecycle experience with a broader parent-company relationship. You can learn more about iT1 on its official website.
