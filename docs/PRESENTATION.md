# Executive presentation walkthrough

## Before the meeting

Open the standalone `Phoenix_Tech_Refresh_Animated_Preview.html` in the browser you will present from. Use a 1440-pixel-or-wider desktop window when practical; 1280 and 1920 layouts were also checked. Enter full-screen mode to give the design room. No internet connection is needed for the homepage or internal demonstrations.

On first opening, use **Essential only** to dismiss the privacy notice. Reopen and change choices using the small privacy button or footer link. Native persistence must be checked once on the actual presentation machine because the build sandbox could not navigate to a normal file origin.

## Suggested six-minute walkthrough

**Opening and positioning.** Pause at the hero: “Your technology’s next chapter. Handled.” Show the approved Premium Corporate logo and the iT1 ownership line. The goal is institutional confidence with personal accountability.

**Explore the motion.** Move the pointer across the hero and click its background away from controls. Show the subtle response, then demonstrate Pause motion and Resume motion. The lifecycle and closing invitation carry the same ribbon language. Reduced-motion users receive a static alternative.

**Breadth without clutter.** Explore two items in the service list, then open one service detail. The selected panel changes immediately rather than sending viewers through a maze of pages.

**Operational clarity.** Click through the asset-lifecycle steps. Explain that the site will sell an understandable process, with explicit responsibilities and evidence—not rely on unsupported certification badges.

**Reporting experience.** In the portal preview, search `DEMO-LT`, then clear the search and try a status filter. Demonstrate the sample CSV export and the Documents and Recovered value tabs. Explicitly say these are synthetic interface examples, not current customer records or financial results.

**The relationship.** Continue through reuse, personal service, the iT1 connection, and industry-specific priorities. The people-first positioning is expressed in copy and project ownership, without fake employee identities or customer quotes.

**The next conversation.** Open the project request. Use clearly fictional review details, advance to the summary, and show that it is prepared—not sent. Do not send an illustrative email to iT1 during the demonstration.

**Governance.** Show Privacy Policy, Terms of Use, and Cookie preferences. Explain the separation between the working privacy controls and policy drafts awaiting legal approval.

## Decisions to request

Approve the homepage direction and copy tone. Identify the real operating entity, Phoenix contact routing, verified certifications, case-study evidence, and photography owner. Select the production CMS/CRM and confirm whether the client portal belongs in the first release or a later phase.
