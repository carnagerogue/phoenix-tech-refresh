## Compliance credentials

For each credential, provide:

- Exact credential/standard name and version, issuing body, certificate number and source document or registry link.
- The certified legal entity, facility/site addresses, covered services and exclusions. Distinguish Phoenix-operated sites from downstream partners and parent-company credentials.
- Issue and expiration dates, verification date, and approved renewal wording.
- Permitted logo artwork and usage rules, approved customer-facing description, and internal reviewer.
- Approved data-handling methods, verification and exception procedures, chain-of-custody records, and a redacted example of documentation clients actually receive.

Do not treat a process aligned with a standard as a certification. Do not assign a partner’s or iT1’s credential to Phoenix without applicable scope evidence. Confirm any regulatory language before use.

## Customer stories — aim for two or three strong examples

One story per project:

| Input | What is needed |
| --- | --- |
| Customer | Approved name, sector, logo permission; otherwise approved anonymized description |
| Starting problem | Refresh, relocation, data-center retirement, surplus inventory, etc. |
| Scope | Device types/count, locations, exclusions, start and closeout dates |
| Recovery percentage | Numerator, denominator, unit (device count/value/weight), definition of recovery, measurement period |
| Timeframe | Calendar or business days; exact start/end milestones; exceptions |
| Value returned | Currency, gross proceeds, service/transport costs, net amount actually returned and payment date |
| Savings | Documented baseline and calculation; distinguish savings from resale proceeds and avoid double counting |
| Supporting evidence | Redacted inventory, final reconciliation, invoices/payment record, project report |
| Customer voice | Verbatim approved quotation, speaker title, permission to publish |
| Approval | Customer contact approval, Phoenix reviewer, date, scope of media consent |

Story structure: **The challenge → What Phoenix did → Verified outcome → Customer perspective.** Lead with one or two defensible metrics. Explain the relevant scope and measurement period beside the result.
