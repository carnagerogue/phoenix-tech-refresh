# Project Brief

## Purpose

Phoenix Tech Refresh is building a new enterprise corporate website for IT asset disposition (ITAD), IT asset remarketing, secure data destruction, reverse logistics, electronics recycling, and technology value recovery.

The website must do more than explain services. It should function as a trust platform, lead-generation engine, compliance resource, and eventually the front door to a secure client reporting portal.

## Business outcomes

1. Generate qualified enterprise RFQs and quote requests.
2. Establish Phoenix Tech Refresh as a credible, audited, enterprise-grade technology lifecycle partner.
3. Differentiate the company from commodity e-waste recyclers through remarketing, value recovery, secure logistics, reporting, and reuse-first outcomes.
4. Give compliance, legal, risk, ESG, IT, procurement, and finance stakeholders enough information to evaluate the company without a sales call.
5. Support existing customers through a future authenticated portal for asset-level status, certificates, recovered value, ESG reporting, and service requests.
6. Support recruiting and employer-brand needs.

## Primary audiences

- Enterprise IT and procurement leaders
- Data-center and infrastructure teams
- Compliance, legal, and risk leaders
- Sustainability / ESG teams
- Finance and asset-management stakeholders
- Channel / reseller partners
- Prospective employees

## Brand position

Phoenix Tech Refresh should appear as a **technology lifecycle and asset-value company first**, not as a local recycler.

The desired brand personality is:

- Modern
- Precise
- Secure
- Enterprise-grade
- Operationally capable
- Technology-forward
- Responsible without looking like a generic environmental brand
- Confident without being loud or gimmicky

## Core positioning pillars

### Secure

Chain of custody, data sanitization/destruction, reporting, auditability, and client confidence.

### Recover

Recover economic value from viable hardware through testing, grading, refurbishment, resale, buyback, and remarketing.

### Extend

Favor useful second life and circularity before end-of-life recycling where appropriate.

### Report

Provide transparent asset-level, compliance, financial, and sustainability reporting.

### Simplify

Make complex multi-site technology refresh, logistics, decommissioning, and disposition programs easier to operate.

## Important content rule

Certification badges, standards, impact metrics, financial/value-recovery claims, customer logos, insurance information, and environmental claims must not be published unless Phoenix Tech Refresh has verified and approved them.

## Current project stage

Discovery and design direction. The full visual system and homepage concept should be approved before production UI implementation begins.
