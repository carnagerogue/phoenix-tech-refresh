## Implemented preference inventory

Storage key: `ptr_privacy_v1`, browser local storage. Duration: 180 days, checked on read. Fields: version, necessary, analytics, marketing, signal, timestamp, expiresAt. No unique visitor ID. No HTTP cookies set by the preview.

Optional categories are off until selected, and remain off when Global Privacy Control or Do Not Track is detected. The panel supports rejection, separate analytics/marketing choices, withdrawal, and reopening from the footer. No tracker is installed, including after acceptance. Malformed, expired, or unrecognized records are ignored. Blocked storage falls back to page-memory behavior.

## Prelaunch gate

Do not simply remove draft labels. First obtain approved policies that match a verified data-flow and vendor inventory; confirm the operator, domain, controller, legal inbox, retention, all state/regional notice requirements, and iT1 affiliate handling. Connect and test rights requests, consent enforcement, marketing choices, and any authentication or forms. Confirm whether a production host logs request data. Then set the actual effective date and publish final route-based legal pages.
